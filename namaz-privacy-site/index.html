Namaz: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Namaz is a prayer app with prayer times, a Hijri calendar, Qibla, a Salah guide, duas, a Quran reader and Ramadan, Eid, Hajj and Umrah guides. This page explains what data the app uses.

In short

  • There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • Your settings, bookmarks, trackers and checklists are saved on your device only. We do not receive them.
  • The app contacts two outside services, only for weather, city search and Quran audio, described below.

Information saved on your device

The app saves your chosen city and location, your madhhab and theme, your own masjid’s namaz times, saved Quran verses, your last-read position, your tasbeeh counts, your prayer and fasting trackers, checklists and calculator entries. This stays on your phone. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Location

If you tap “Use my GPS location”, the app asks for your phone’s location permission. Your position is used on your device to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and the Hijri moon and date. The app does not send your location to us. The app only uses location while it is open.

To show the weather, the app sends your latitude and longitude to the weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo’s search service. Open-Meteo’s own privacy information applies to those requests.

Quran audio

When you play a recitation, your device downloads the audio file from the Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network, AlQuran Cloud). As with any website or file download, that service can see your device’s IP address and which file you requested. Its own privacy information applies.

Notifications

In the Android app, prayer alerts are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to any server. You can turn notifications off in the app’s Settings or in Android’s settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
